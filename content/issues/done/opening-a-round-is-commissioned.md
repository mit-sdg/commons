# Open each round within its run, and let the next press finish it

## Decision at completion

Decided on 2026-09-23. A round is published within its run by one Publishing action, and Publishing's own guards refuse a closed run, a second open round, and a round that already ran. Commissioning, Locking and Linking leave the opening. The request says which piles the round opens on. Every state an opening can stop in is one the dashboard shows and the ordinary next press of Open finishes. A declined press answers its word as data; the other live endpoints still answer a category. The dashboard gains one quiet line about the room, read from a new concept, Attending.

## Resolution at completion

Publishing gained parts. `publishWithin` publishes an edition within an open whole, and the same insert that opens the part records its whole, so a round belongs to its run from the moment it exists. Its guards refuse a closed whole (`WHOLE_CLOSED`), a second open part (`PART_OPEN`), and a material already published within the whole (`PART_DONE`). Three partial unique indexes hold the same rules across processes: one open edition per material, one open part per whole, and one part per material in each whole. `_parts` and `_openPart` answer a run's rounds and its open round, and `_edition` carries the whole.

`/live/relays/open-round` takes `{ run, leg, picked }` and writes twice: the round, published within its run, then its presentation, computed from the leg's question and the piles the request picks. Between the two writes the round is opening: the run read names it as `opening`, phones stay on "Waiting for the next round.", and a begin is refused. Once its presentation stands, the round is open to the room. Open can be pressed again safely. A press for the round already open answers that round and ensures again a standing pile missing from its wall, and a press for a round left opening captures its presentation and answers it. Close round closes a round left opening, which then counts as run and shows on phones as a round that ran. Close run closes the run's open or opening round first, and answers a run already closed with the run. An opening whose run closed between the two writes is declined `CLOSED`, with nothing captured.

A press that cannot open its round is answered as data, `{ declined: WORD }`, with the facts its sentence names: `LEG_NOT_FOUND`, `CLOSED`, `ROUND_DONE` with the round, `SOURCE_UNRUN` and `SOURCE_OPEN` with the round it takes from, `ROUND_OPEN` with the round in the way, `NOTHING_PICKED`, and `PILE_GONE`. The dashboard says the word's sentence from `frontend/src/components/live/refusals.ts`, such as "Close round 3 first."

Model seats are begun by the participant worker. Each pass begins every seat of a round open to the room that the seat has not begun, so a begin lost to a fault or a restart is made on the next pass, and no seat is begun on a round still opening. A seat taken while a round is open still begins that round at once.

Commissioning, Locking and Linking are off the opening. The run lock, Unlock, and the dashboard's guess that a run was left locked are gone; Locking stays for a wall's placing asks, and Commissioning for the model's sort work. Every reader of a round's run reads Publishing's parts.

The migration `20260923T000100-rounds-within-runs` records each round's run as its whole from the old links, closes each round edition that no run holds (left by an interrupted opening) and each round still open in a closed run, then builds the indexes. When a database breaks one of Publishing's rules in a way only a person can settle, such as two open editions of one material outside any run, startup stops with a diagnostic that names the editions, and nothing is written. Old links and run locks stay in place, and nothing reads them.

The dashboard sends the picked piles with each press. While a round is left opening it says "Round 2 is opening." beside Close round 2, and Open finishes it. While the wall a round takes from is still being sorted, it says "Round 1 is still being sorted: 10 cards are not on a pile yet." and keeps Open pressable; the round opens on the cards placed so far. A round closed before it opened reads "Round 1 closed before it opened." Every screen takes the open and opening rounds from the run read's `openRound` and `opening` alone, so a read taken during an opening never shows the new round as run. No screen offers Unlock or mentions a lock.

Attending counts the devices on the participant link, whether phones, laptops or tablets, and shows nothing on their screens. After a page on a relay run renders an arrive's answer, it checks in with its own request, `/live/p/attend`, holding the round it shows or nothing while it waits. Arrive never writes the attendance, so a phone's poll never waits on the room. Attending keeps each check-in to a grain of twenty seconds, and a page that closes sends `/live/p/leave`. The run read carries the room: how many devices were heard in the last minute, and how many of them hold the open round. Model seats never check in, so the room never counts them. The dashboard's line says "40 here" while no round is open. While one is open, it says nothing when every device here is on it and "2 of 40 here are still waiting for round 2" when some are not. It stays silent for the first ten seconds after the dashboard first sees the round open, since a phone reports a round only on the poll after the one that brought it.

Two limits stay open, each in its own record: [a standing pile lost after the capture has no repair on the dashboard, and the Open answer waits for its writes](../open/a-standing-pile-lost-after-the-capture-has-no-repair-on-the-dashboard.md), and the order the picked piles carry in, since [decided as the closed wall's](picked-piles-carry-in-the-order-the-closed-wall-shows.md).

## Verification at completion

`tests/concepts/publishing.test.ts` covers every `publishWithin` refusal and which one wins when several apply, one open part per whole, one part per material, `_parts` in publication order, and concurrent parts within one process. It also races instances on one database, whose losers are refused by the guard or the index rather than faulted. `tests/concepts/attending.test.ts` covers the grain, a changed holding recorded at once, leave and a second leave refused, `_present`, expiry, and check-ins racing across instances.

`tests/app/relay-opening.test.ts` covers a round between its two writes (not open to phones until its presentation stands, and declined `CLOSED` with nothing captured when the run closes in between). It also covers the press made again: it answers the open round and puts back a pile a fault left off, it finishes a round left opening, and a press whose reads straddle the capture is answered at once. The same file covers Close round on a round left opening, Close run on a run already closed, the Live list and relay page read through parts, and two presses racing to Publishing's guard on one and on two engines. `tests/app/relay-refusals.test.ts` covers each declined word with its facts, picks refused before anything opens when they are not a list of piles, and the source wall's pins never standing in for the request's picks. `tests/app/relay-races.test.ts` covers a close during an opening, which cannot leave the round open, and concurrent presses, which leave one round open and a run that reads. `tests/app/rounds-within-runs-migration.test.ts` covers tied rounds, orphans, a closed run's open round, running the migration twice, and each database that stops startup. `tests/app/participant-worker.test.ts` covers seating from the worker: no seat begun on a round still opening, every seat begun on the pass after the capture, a lost begin made again, and a dismissed or handed-in seat never begun. `tests/app/relay-attendance.test.ts` covers the check-in, an arrive at its usual speed while a check-in hangs, a face kept when Attending faults, one write inside the grain, the room on the run read, leave, and model seats never counted.

In the frontend, `frontend/src/components/live/run-relay-board.test.ts` covers the piles Open sends, the round Open offers (a round left opening first), the strip on a read taken during an opening, what a lost race and a failed Close round say, and the line for a source wall still being sorted. `rounds.test.ts` beside it covers which round a phone and the strip show open or opening, `room-line.test.ts` covers the line and its ten-second wait, `refusals.test.ts` covers the declined sentences, and `frontend/src/lib/poll.test.ts` covers when a page checks in. The robustness scenario `tests/robustness/scenarios/r6-open-race.ts` presses two rounds from two sessions a hundred times and checks that one round opens each time, every run read answers, and the loser's round opens after the close. The live e2e specs under `tests/e2e/` open every round through `/live/relays/open-round`, and `relay-source-context.spec.ts` opens a vote and a list on the picks the request carries. Beside the tests, fault-injection runs on the production build slowed, refused, faulted, or restarted each stage of an opening, and had two dashboards press together on one or two engines. Every case ended with every open round reachable, never two open rounds, and never a round open to phones without its question, and after each interruption the next press opened or finished the round.

One part of the acceptance condition is met in a narrower form. Two presses that pass the server's reads together meet at Publishing's guard, and the loser is answered with the category, `CONFLICT`, not the word, since the engine's HTTP boundary answers a concept's refusal with its category only. The dashboard then reads the run and says what stands: nothing more when the round it asked for is open or opening, "Close round 2 first." when another round is, and "The run is closed." when the run has closed. In a hundred trials of r6, both presses passed the reads together every time, so the loser always heard `CONFLICT`.
