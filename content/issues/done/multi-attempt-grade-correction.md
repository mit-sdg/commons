# Correct grades when learners have several attempts

## Resolution at completion

Assessment identity includes the exact submission attempt. Staff select one attempt and edit one version-checked draft. New attempts receive separate assessments; retracting and releasing a correction preserves earlier releases inside the same assessment.

## Decision at completion

Keep one assessment per learner, item, and evidence attempt. Distinct work and corrections are separate concepts in the interface; no attempt supplies an overall or current competency grade.

## Verification at completion

Grading and course wire tests exercise separate attempts, corrections, and historical rubric editions. The staff editor provides the retraction and release workflow.
