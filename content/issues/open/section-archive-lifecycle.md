# Decide how sections leave active use

## Current behavior

Sections can be created and edited but never archived. Seats may continue to
refer to every section ever created.

## Unresolved decision

Define what archiving a section means for active, pending, and dropped seats,
and whether a section may be restored or only replaced.

## Acceptance condition

The Rostering specification includes the settled lifecycle and tests cover
every seat standing when a section leaves active use.
