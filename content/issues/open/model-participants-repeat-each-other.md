# Invited model participants answer alike

## Current behavior

Ten model participants invited on one round hand in answer sets of which a
third or more are word for word another participant's, on the short-answer
games most of all; on "Fix the spec" most restate the prompt instead of
rewriting it. The passage deals each participant a stance and an angle by its
identity, asks for the length the question wants, and tells it not to echo
the prompt: on the same bank, duplicates fell from 19 to 3 of 50 answer
sets, the restated prompt from 7 to none, and ten seats on "Name the
activity" hand in seven distinct words on Gemini 3.7 Flash and ten on 3.8.
The rest is what the passage alone cannot reach on the one-word round.
