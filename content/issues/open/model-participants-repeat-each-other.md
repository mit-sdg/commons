# Invited model participants answer alike

## Current behavior

Ten model participants invited on one round hand in answer sets of which a
third or more are word for word another participant's, on the short-answer
games most of all; on "Fix the spec" most restate the prompt instead of
rewriting it. The passage now deals each participant a stance by its identity
and tells it not to echo the prompt: on the same bank, duplicates fell from
19 to 3 of 50 answer sets and the restated prompt from 7 to 2 of 10. The
rest is what the stances alone cannot reach.

## Unresolved decision

Whether the passage alone can make forty seats read like forty phones, or
whether the dashboard should invite fewer seats and say what they are for.

## Acceptance condition

Ten participants on "Name the activity" hand in at least eight distinct
answers, and none on "Fix the spec" restates the given sentence.
