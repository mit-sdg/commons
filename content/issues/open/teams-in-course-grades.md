# Grade team work by team

## Current behavior

Commons has no teams in its grading. Course grades treat the assignments of
kind Final project as team work and count each member's own assessment. Staff
correct a member who did less than their share with an override.

## Unresolved decision

Define what a team is in a course (who forms it, whether it changes during the
term, which assignments it owns), whether one assessment is shared by the
team, and how a per-member adjustment is recorded and shown.

## Acceptance condition

Application tests form a team, grade one team deliverable, and read the same
rating on each member's course grade, with a per-member adjustment that names
its reason.
