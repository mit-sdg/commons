# Sorting a wall by hand is drag-only

## Current behavior

On the dashboard a card is placed in a pile, sent back to the tray, or used
to open a new pile only by dragging it, and two piles are merged only by
dragging one onto the other. A keyboard reaches every pile, the spread, the
rename, and the pick, but no card: a staff member who cannot drag cannot sort
by hand, and the model's sorting is the only other way.

## Unresolved decision

What the keyboard's sorting reads like on a wall of stacks: a focused card
with a "Move to…" list of the wall's piles and the tray, or a pile that takes
a card by name, and whether the projector's big wall needs it at all.

## Acceptance condition

Every hand move the wall page offers — place, return to tray, open a pile
with a card, merge — can be made from the keyboard alone on the dashboard,
with the move announced.
