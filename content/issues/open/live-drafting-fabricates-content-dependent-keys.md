# Ground content-dependent drafting requests instead of inventing answer keys

## Current behavior

A drafting request whose right answers depend on material the reasoner has
never seen — "a quiz on our security policy", "a quiz about today's all-hands"
— comes back as a confidently specific draft whose expected answers are
invented (a "16 characters" password minimum, "Sales exceeded targets by 15%").
The clarifying-question gate covers only quiz-versus-survey ambiguity, so
nothing asks the author for the source material, and a hurried author could
adopt and launch a quiz whose answer key is wrong. The drafting surface does
put every expected answer before the author's eyes prior to adoption, which is
the current safeguard.

The same reading applies to hostile requests: the parse partition and the
insistence keep every reply inside the draft/question/stall contract, but a
request written to smuggle text into quiz content can succeed at the content
level, because content is the author's to review.

## Unresolved decision

Whether the contract should grow a second asking ground — request material the
draft depends on before drafting — or whether authors' pre-adoption review
remains the designed safeguard, stated in the drafting surface's own words.

## Acceptance condition

Either the drafting line can ask for missing source material and drafts from
what it is given, with the stress personas re-run to show fabrication gone, or
a recorded decision names pre-adoption review as the safeguard and the
drafting surface says so where the author reads the draft.
