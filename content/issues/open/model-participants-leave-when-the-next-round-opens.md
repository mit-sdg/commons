# Keep invited model participants in the room for the next round

## Current behavior

Inviting model participants begins a response for each under the round that
is open. When the next round opens, no response begins for them: the wall of
the new round has no model cards, and the dashboard's count of model
participants reads zero until staff invite again.

## Unresolved decision

Whether an invited seat belongs to the run, so every round that opens begins
a response for each participant invited earlier in the run, or whether seats
are invited per round as now and the dashboard should say so. The design's
phone analogy — a participant that hands in like any phone, on its own clock —
favors the run.

## Acceptance condition

Three model participants invited on round one hand in on round two without a
second invitation, and the dashboard counts three throughout.
