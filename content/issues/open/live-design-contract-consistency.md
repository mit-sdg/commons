# Align the live design account with its owned behavior

## Current behavior

The application binding for Guiding omits questionnaire reference selections
and category definitions and summaries. Lockable omits the Reasoning askings
held while their replies are applied. Guidance descriptions still imply that
all library documents reach every drafter, despite explicit selections.

Insisting's rule describes a bound over an aim, while a new insistence can
start for the same aim after settlement. Patience counts complaints within
one insistence, not all attempts over the aim's lifetime.

Commissioning.report returns the retained first receipt's successful and
account values, which may differ from later inputs. Its action text does not
explicitly identify those retained values in the return.

The Principles do not yet exercise Guiding's selections, Commissioning's
receipt-before-association and history expiry, Reasoning's follow-ups, or
Drafting's inherited context. Drafting's describe and open action text also
omits the context retained by their implementation and State rule.

## Unresolved decision

Complete the binding owners and operation accounts without changing existing
behavior. Keep reader access and whether guidance is consulted in the
application; Guiding stores and selects it by use. Separate concept invariants
from the Mongo deployment mechanisms that realize them.

## Acceptance condition

Application types enumerate every identity owner actually supplied to those
roles. Reference descriptions consistently require explicit selection. The
Insisting bound names its lifecycle, Commissioning's report names the retained
return, and the Principles exercise the meaningful new distinctions. Design
checks and regenerated artifacts agree with the corrected account.
