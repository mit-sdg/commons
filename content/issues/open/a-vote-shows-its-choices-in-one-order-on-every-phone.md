# A vote shows its choices in one order on every phone

## Current behavior

A vote round built from picked piles lists its choices fullest pile first on every phone, the order the closed wall showed them. A choice listed first is chosen a little more often, and more so on a phone where later choices sit below the fold, so the idea the room already favoured gets that lift. A list round's parts follow the same order, and staff cannot reorder them before the round opens.

## Unresolved decision

Whether each phone shows a vote's choices in its own shuffled order, with the wall and the tallies keeping one order for the room, and whether staff can reorder the picked piles before opening, which would give a list's parts an order chosen on purpose.

## Acceptance condition

Whatever is decided, the order each phone shows a vote's choices in and the order a list's parts open in are stated in the relay's specification and read back by tests on the phone and on the server.
